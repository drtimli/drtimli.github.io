04 / Questions

Before we get started.

Can AI work with our existing systems?

We assess available interfaces, data sources, and workflows, then design integration around your environment. Feasibility and scope depend on the systems and access available.

How do you approach data privacy?

We discuss data sensitivity, permissions, retention, and applicable requirements before choosing tools or architecture. Access controls, provider settings, and human review are considered as part of the design.

What deployment options are available?

Depending on your requirements, options may include cloud services, your own infrastructure, or a hybrid setup. We review security, operations, performance, and cost trade-offs together.

How do we get started?

Send a short description of your challenge and current environment. An initial conversation can help identify whether an assessment, architecture review, or implementation engagement is the right next step.
